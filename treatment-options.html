Concerning bleeding comes before hormone education.

New, heavy, persistent or otherwise concerning bleeding should route to safety and clinical review before continuing hormone education. Seek prompt guidance from an appropriate licensed clinician.

Hormone & BHRT center

FDA-approved bioidentical hormones and compounded BHRT—without a winner.

The term bioidentical describes hormones that are chemically identical to hormones the body produces. It does not by itself indicate whether a product is FDA-approved or compounded.

✓

FDA-approved bioidentical hormone therapy

Standardized products reviewed by the FDA for quality, safety and effectiveness for specific uses. Form, dose, benefits and risks still require individualized clinician guidance.

⚗

Compounded BHRT

Customized preparations made by compounding pharmacies for specific clinical circumstances. They are not FDA-approved as finished products and require careful assessment and monitoring.

⚖

Neither category is presented as universally superior or inferior. A licensed clinician can explain relevant evidence, uncertainties, formulation differences, personal risk factors and appropriate monitoring.
