Estrogen Patch (Estradiol)

Hormonal · Transdermal delivery

Approved Edit Treatment
Overview
CategoryHormonal Therapy
MechanismTransdermal estradiol replacement, systemic absorption via patch
Typical Dosage0.025–0.1 mg/day, applied twice weekly
Onset2–4 weeks for symptom relief
Clinical Evidence
Vasomotor Symptom ReductionGrade A
Sleep Quality ImprovementGrade A
Bone Density PreservationGrade B
Safety & Contraindications
Black Box WarningCardiovascular / VTE Risk
