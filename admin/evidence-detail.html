Paced Breathing for Hot Flashes

Edit ReferenceLink to ContentArchive
Source & Citation
Publisher / Body
North American Menopause Society (NAMS)
Full Clinical Citation
"Nonhormonal management of menopause-associated vasomotor symptoms: 2023 position statement of The North American Menopause Society." Menopause, Vol. 30, No. 9, pp. 885-906.
Evidence Summary

This randomized controlled baseline study demonstrates that behavioral paced deep-breathing exercises practiced 15 minutes twice daily reduce hot flash intensity. Controlled inhalation and exhalation stabilize autonomic fluctuations and offset norepinephrine surges that constrict the thermoregulatory window.

Applicable Topics & Patient Audiences
Hot FlashesPerimenopauseBehavioral Therapy
Identified Limitations
Self-reported symptom diaries introduce subjective reporting bias. Compliance tracking for paced breathing remains difficult without integrated medical biosensors.
