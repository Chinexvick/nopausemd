Safety is built into the journey

AI can guide the conversation. It cannot practice medicine.

The AI Health Coach does not diagnose, prescribe, change medication or hormone doses, or replace a clinician. Deterministic safety escalation flags concerning inputs and directs you toward appropriate human review.
