Delete Your Account

How to request deletion of your CliniPause account and data, in the app or by email.

Option 1: Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the CliniPause app and sign in.
  2. Go to Me.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Yes, delete my account.

Your account is deleted immediately after confirmation.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email info@clinipause.com from the email address on your CliniPause account and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Profile and account information (name, email)Deleted
Health data: symptoms, assessment answers, Health Score, tracking history, your planDeleted
AI coach conversation historyDeleted
Clinician messages and consultation recordsDeleted, except where your clinician is required by law to retain visit records
Subscription and billing recordsRetained only as long as required for tax, accounting, and fraud-prevention law
Crash and diagnostic logs (no personal data collected)Not linked to you; nothing to delete

Except where we're legally required to keep certain records, all of your personal data is deleted or anonymized within 30 days of your request.

Questions

Contact us at info@clinipause.com with any questions about this process.
