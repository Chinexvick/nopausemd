COMMON QUESTIONS

Have Questions? We've Got Answers.

What is CliniPause?
CliniPause provides personalized healthcare and wellness support focused on perimenopause, menopause, hormone health, and overall wellbeing.
What symptoms can CliniPause help me with?
We support common symptoms including hot flashes, night sweats, mood changes, sleep issues, brain fog, weight changes, and hormonal shifts.
How do I get started?
Book a consultation with our team to discuss your symptoms, health history, and goals so we can build a personalized care plan.
Can I purchase CliniPause products online?
Yes — visit our Shop page to browse wellness products and the handbook, available for direct purchase online.
Is CliniPause only for women experiencing menopause?
Not at all. We support women at every stage — from early perimenopause symptoms through post-menopause wellness.
Do I need a consultation before purchasing products?
No consultation is required to purchase our wellness products or handbook, though we recommend one for personalized guidance.
