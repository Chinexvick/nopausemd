Common questions

Straight answers about how CliniPause works.

Is CliniPause a medical practice?

No. CliniPause is a digital health platform providing education, personalized wellness guidance, and symptom tracking. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace care from a licensed clinician.

What does the AI Health Coach actually do?

The AI coach answers questions about your symptoms, explains treatment options, and helps you prepare for your clinician. It cannot diagnose conditions or prescribe treatment.

Can I use CliniPause alongside my current doctor?

Absolutely—that's exactly how it's designed. Many members share their Health Score with their existing clinicians.

Are the supplements safe to use with medications?

Our Supplement Interaction Checker reviews your current stack against your health profile. Always consult your physician first.

What happens to my health data?

Your data is private by design. Sensitive categories have additional protections, and you can export or delete your data at any time.

How much does CliniPause cost?

The guided assessment and Midlife Health Score are free. Membership is a monthly subscription—cancel anytime.
