Privacy Policy

How CliniPause collects, uses, and protects your information across our app and website.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

CliniPause ("CliniPause," "we," "us," or "our") provides an educational and wellness app and website for people navigating perimenopause and menopause, together with optional access to independent, licensed clinicians for consultations and video visits. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the CliniPause mobile app and the clinipausemd.com website.

CliniPause provides education and personalized wellness guidance. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace care from a licensed clinician. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call your local emergency number.

1. Information We Collect

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalize the app: symptom tracking, your Health Score, your weekly plan, and relief tools.
  • To power the AI health coach, which responds to your questions with educational information. The AI coach is not medical advice and does not replace your clinician; it is designed to direct you to a clinician or local emergency services for anything urgent or outside its scope.
  • To let you book, message, and meet by video with independent licensed clinicians.
  • To send you reminders, account, and service notifications.
  • To process payments and manage your subscription.
  • To maintain the security of the app and diagnose technical problems.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your health information for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. How We Share Your Information

We share information only with service providers who help us run CliniPause, under agreements that limit their use of your data to providing their service to us:

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider, which stores your account and health data.
  • OpenAI — powers the AI coach. Messages you send the AI coach are shared with OpenAI only after you've consented to use that feature.
  • Stripe — processes subscription payments. Stripe receives your payment details directly; we only receive your subscription status.
  • Twilio — provides the video infrastructure for clinician video visits. Visits pass through Twilio in real time and are not recorded or stored by CliniPause.
  • Expo — delivers push notifications to your device.
  • Sentry — helps us detect and fix crashes and errors; personal data collection is disabled in our Sentry configuration.
  • Independent licensed clinicians you choose to consult with, so they can provide you care.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of CliniPause or our users, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with notice to you).

4. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access and export — you can export a copy of your data from Me → Export My Data in the app.
  • Deletion — you can delete your account and associated data at any time from Me → Delete Account in the app, or by emailing us.
  • Notifications — you can manage push notification and reminder preferences in the app's settings.
  • AI coach consent — the AI coach only sends your messages to our AI provider after you consent to that feature; you can decline and still use the rest of the app.
  • If you are a California resident, you may have additional rights under the CCPA, including the right to know, delete, and opt out of certain sharing. We do not sell personal information.

5. Data Security and Retention

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for all data sent between the app and our servers, and access controls to limit who can see your information. We retain your data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data, except where we are required to retain it by law.

6. Children's Privacy

CliniPause is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. International Users

CliniPause currently operates in the United States. If you access the app from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above, and if changes are material, we'll provide additional notice in the app.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at info@clinipause.com.
